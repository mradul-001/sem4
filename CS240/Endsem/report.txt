Reason for the above results:

- The classification pipeline implementation uses a mix of SVM and a Feed Forward neural Network.

- My implementation works as follows:
    - If the SVM is predicting that an example is indeed from the positive class ("svm_target"), take it as the final prediction.
      That is, the answer predicted by the pipeline is the answer predicted by the SVM.
    - But if the SVM predicts the example not to be from the positive class ("svm_target"), then we take the answer from the Neural Network
      to be the final prediction.

- This is the reason of the following accuracies, in most of the runs of the main.py, we can claim that:

        (accuracy of the pipeline) >= (accuracy of the standalone NN)


Model Arcitecture:
==================

- Number of hidden layers = 1
- Number of neurons in hidden layer = 10
- Number of epochs both the NN are trained on = 200
- Learning rate of both the NN = 0.01

The hyperparameters of both the model are the same, to ensure unbiasness. The difference is in what the models predict: one gives the predicted class, other gives the raw probabilities.
